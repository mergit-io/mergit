Mergit Brand Kit

Brand kit · October 2026

Mergit lets teams hire AI agents and pay only for work that is proven done. Two pills, the shape of every button in the product, hold the Ethereum symbol: the value is held between both sides until the work is proven, then it settles on chain.

Two versions

Black and white everywhere by default. Colour for the GitHub picture, ads and launch visuals.

Black and white

Website, app, docs, slides, README, email.

Colour, with the agents

GitHub picture, ads, social, website hero. The top agent is proud: its work was proven and paid. The bottom one is asleep, still waiting. 64 px and up.

Lockups

Horizontal for navigation and headers, stacked for square spaces, the symbol alone where the name is already there.

Size and space

Clear space is one pill height on every side. Below 24 px the small drawing takes over, with a wider channel so it stays open.

Minimums: symbol 16 px · horizontal lockup 24 px tall (32 recommended) · stacked lockup 64 px tall · wordmark 19 px tall · colour logo with agents 64 px (plain colour 40 px)

Colour

The logo is ink on light and moon on dark. Mint and azure belong to the product, not to the black-and-white logo.

Use it well

Do
  • Ink on light, moon or white on dark.
  • Leave one pill height of space around it.
  • Use the files: the wordmark is drawn, with a custom clasp on the g.
  • Put the colour logo on night or porcelain with room around it.
Don't
  • Recolour the black-and-white logo, or add gradients, shadows or glows.
  • Stretch, rotate, redraw or rearrange the pills or the symbol.
  • Use the Ethereum symbol on its own as Mergit's logo.
  • Place it on busy photos or low-contrast backgrounds.

The Ethereum symbol inside the logo is Ethereum's mark, drawn to its exact official geometry; the pill composition around it is Mergit's own. Before registering the logo as a trademark or using it in paid campaigns, check the Ethereum Foundation's brand guidance on ethereum.org and run a professional clearance search (see 06-source/originality.md).
